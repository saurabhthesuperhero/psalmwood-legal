Privacy Policy: Psalmwood

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Psalmwood: Bible Verse Widget ("the app", package com.saikaizen.psalmwood) shows daily Bible verses on your home screen, lock screen and in the app. It is published by Saikaizen Innovations ("we", "us"). There is no account and no advertising. This page says exactly what the app does with data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your highlights, bookmarks, notes, prayer journal (including answered prayers), reading-plan progress, memory verses, days kept and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive them. They are included in Android's own device backup if you have it turned on, and in an export file only when you create one yourself.
  • Widgets, the daily lock-screen wallpaper and the live wallpaper are created on your phone. Setting a wallpaper uses Android's wallpaper permission; no image is uploaded.
  • Reminders and the daily verse notification are local notifications scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • The Bible text, art and audio voices used by the app are stored in the app or on your phone; reading and listening do not send what you read anywhere.

What is sent over the internet, and to whom

  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. The app sends usage events (for example: the daily verse was opened, a widget was added, a plan day was finished, a paywall was shown, a purchase or trial started) with device information (phone model, Android version, language, country), an app instance identifier and approximate location derived from your IP address. Events never contain your notes, prayers or journal text. Processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.
  • Feedback you send from Settings > Feedback & report: the message you write, the category you chose, the app version and phone model, and an e-mail address only if you type one. It is stored in Google Cloud Firestore so we can read and answer it.
  • Google Play Billing handles Psalmwood Premium purchases and trials. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock Premium; payment details stay with Google.

We do not sell personal data, we do not show ads, and we do not share data with data brokers.

Children

Psalmwood is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and data deletion

You can delete all on-device data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. To ask about or delete feedback you sent, write to us.
